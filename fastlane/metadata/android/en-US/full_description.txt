Neuro Granny helps you remember passwords, PINs, bicycle lock combinations, door codes and other things you only need occasionally.

Instead of looking up a code, practice recalling it when Neuro Granny reminds you. You can set fixed review intervals or use adaptive scheduling based on your recall performance.

Features:
* Persistent recall reminders
* Generated geometric patterns or your own photos as visual cues
* Fixed or adaptive review schedules
* Review history and statistics
* Local SQLite import and export
* Recoverable secrets or salted Argon2id verify-only storage
* Eight interface languages

The app works offline. No accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics or internet permission are required.

Privacy and recovery: Recoverable secrets are stored as readable text in the app's private SQLite database. Database exports are unencrypted and may include sensitive information. Verify-only secrets cannot be recovered. Keep a separate backup of important credentials; this app is a memory-training aid, not a replacement for a password manager.
